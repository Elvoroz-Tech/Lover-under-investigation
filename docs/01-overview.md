# 01 · Overview

**Working name:** Lover Under Investigation 🕵️ (changeable via `NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_NAME`).

A relationship roast disguised as an official investigation. You pick a funny "crime" your partner committed, the site
instantly issues an official-looking certificate, and you send them the link. They open a sealed case file, see what
they've been accused of, laugh, and file a counter-case against you.

## Audience
Gen Z (roughly 16–28), mobile-first, in couples or close friendships. Heavy WhatsApp / Instagram / Snapchat users who
hate long flows, signups and forms. Indian audience is a strong secondary focus (Diwali reference, "Desi" teaser, Hinglish).

## Core principles
1. **The certificate is the product.** Not a quiz, not an inbox, not a compatibility test.
2. **One tap to a result.** Picking a crime *is* the whole input. Names are optional and collapsed.
3. **The share is the main action.** "Send to Partner" is the largest, glowing button.
4. **Don't explain the joke before the click.** Link previews show only "a case has been opened", never the crime.
5. **Zero-storage privacy.** No accounts, no database of cases. The case lives inside the link.
6. **Honest anonymity.** The sender's name is not in the link unless they explicitly switch on "sign it".
7. **Never boring.** Every screen has motion: entrances, springs, stamps, confetti, marquees, tilt, envelopes.

## The viral loop
```
Sender picks crime → certificate → "Send to Partner" (teaser text + link + image)
   → Partner sees teaser preview ("someone opened a case against you 💀")
   → opens link → breaks the seal → certificate reveal + confetti
   → "File a counter-case" → becomes a sender → …
```
